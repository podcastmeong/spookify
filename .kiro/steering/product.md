# Spookify — Product Steering

## What it is
Spookify is a free, client-side web app that turns any photo into a Halloween
portrait and writes a short horror story inspired by it. No account, no
upload to a server, no cost. Built for the Kiroween 2026 hackathon
(Devpost), Costume Contest category.

## Who it is for
Anyone who wants a spooky profile picture, a Halloween party invitation
image, or a creepy story to share — in under a minute, from their phone or
laptop.

## Core promise
1. Upload a photo (or use the built-in sample scene).
2. Pick one of six Halloween effects — the result is visible instantly.
3. Get an original short horror story (title + 4 paragraphs) generated
   procedurally in the browser.
4. Download the finished portrait as a PNG and copy the story to share.

## Principles
- Everything runs in the browser. No backend, no tracking, no data leaves
  the device. A photo never leaves the user's machine.
- Instant feedback: every control updates the canvas immediately.
- Fun first: effects must be visibly different from each other, not subtle
  filters. A "Blood Moon" portrait must look like a blood moon night.
- English UI and stories (the contest and its judges are English-speaking).
- Accessible: keyboard-usable buttons, alt text, sensible contrast.
